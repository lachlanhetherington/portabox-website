Questions

Self storage in Robina: your questions, answered

Can’t see yours? Ring the depot — someone who actually does the runs will answer.

How much does self storage in Robina cost?

From $209 a month for the 10 m³ container, $219 for the 19 m³ and the 25 m³ — the same rate anywhere we deliver, because you are paying for the container rather than for the postcode.

Robina is 50 km from the Gilberton depot, so the delivery leg is costed by distance. The instant quote gives you the figure before you commit, and the pricing page shows the arithmetic per cubic metre.

Do you deliver to Robina?

Yes. Robina is served from our Brisbane depot at 9 Citywest Court, Gilberton QLD 4208, 50 km away by road. Put your postcode into the box at the top of this page and it will confirm it.

How long before you can deliver?

It depends on what is free on the Gilberton run that week, so the honest answer is that we confirm the date and the window with you before the truck is booked rather than promising one here. Ring 07 3053 8770 and they will tell you what is open.

Do I need a council permit in Robina?

On your own driveway or in your yard, no. If the container has to sit on the road or the nature strip, most councils want a permit and the rules differ council by council — tell us the address when you book and we will tell you what applies at yours.

What size container do I need?

A 10 m³ holds roughly a one-bedroom flat, a 19 m³ a two-bedroom home, and a 25 m³ a three-bedroom house including the shed. The container sizes page lists the internal dimensions and door clearances.

Can I keep it at my place instead of your yard?

Yes, and most people in Robina do. It sits on your driveway with your own lock on it and you open it whenever you like. If you would rather it went away, we collect it into Gilberton — see the facility.
