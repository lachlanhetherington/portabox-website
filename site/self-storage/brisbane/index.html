Questions

Self storage in Brisbane: your questions, answered

Can’t see yours? Call one of our team — someone who actually does the runs will answer.

Call 1800 467 637
How much does self storage in Brisbane cost?

From $209 a month for the 10 m³ container, $219 for the 19 m³ and the 25 m³. That is the container itself, not a share of a warehouse — which is why it is worth comparing on the price per cubic metre rather than the headline rate.

Delivery inside 35 km of the Gilberton depot is included. Past that it is costed by distance, and the instant quote will tell you before you commit.

Do you deliver to my suburb in Brisbane?

We deliver across Brisbane, including Capalaba, Yatala, Brendale, Aspley and the rest of the list above. Put your postcode into the box at the top of this page and it will tell you which depot covers you.

What size container do I need?

A 10 m³ holds about a one-bedroom flat, a 19 m³ a two-bedroom home, and a 25 m³ a three-bedroom house including the shed. If you are between two sizes, the container sizes page lists the internal dimensions and door clearances.

Can I keep the container at my place, or does it go to your facility?

Either. Leave it on the driveway and open it whenever you like, or we collect it into the Gilberton yard once you have loaded it. The rate per cubic metre is the same both ways — see how it works.

How soon can you deliver in Brisbane?

We confirm the date and the window with you before the truck is booked, so the honest answer is that it depends on the run. Ring the Brisbane depot on 07 3053 8770 and they will tell you what is free this week.

Do I need a council permit to put a container on the street?

If it sits on your own driveway or yard, no. If it has to sit on the road or the nature strip, most councils want a permit, and the rules differ council by council. Tell us the address when you book and we will tell you what applies.
