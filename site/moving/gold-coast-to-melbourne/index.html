Gold Coast to Melbourne: your questions, answered

How much does it cost to move from Gold Coast to Melbourne?

The line haul for one container Gold Coast to Melbourne is $2,900. On top of that sit the delivery legs at each end — $149 per container per leg — and the monthly rate for however long you keep it, from $209.

That is the whole of it. The instant quote adds it up for your two addresses and shows you the breakdown before you commit.

How long does a move from Gold Coast to Melbourne take?

It runs on a scheduled line haul rather than a dedicated truck, which is what keeps it cheaper than a removalist. We give you the date when you book, after we have the two addresses — we do not quote a transit time here that we might not make.

Do I have to pack it all in one day?

No, and that is the point of a container. It sits on your driveway in Gold Coast for as long as you need, you load it at ground level, and it leaves when you say. No removalist waiting with the clock running.

What if the new place is not ready?

Then it does not have to go straight there. We can hold the container at Laverton North until the Melbourne end is ready, and you pay the monthly rate while it sits. Nobody opens it — your lock stays on.

Is one container enough?

A 25 m³ holds a three-bedroom house including the shed. If you are over that, two containers cost two line hauls, so it is worth checking the sizes before you book.
